## Cheat sheet (the big picture)

| Food | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|
| **Eggs** | From kosher birds only; normal chicken eggs need no questions |
| **Fertilized eggs** | Any blood spot → the whole egg is forbidden (custom); check when you see it opened |
| **Stamped eggs** | Unfertilized: just remove a blood spot; no check required |
| **Honey** | Kosher (bees only store nectar, or a verse permits it); strain out bee parts |
| **Royal jelly** | Disputed; fine in honey or capsules; propolis and pollen permitted |

## 1. Kosher eggs

- "What comes from the impure is impure" (Bekhorot 5b): eggs of non-kosher birds are forbidden.
- Ordinary chicken eggs: buy from anyone (non-kosher bird eggs aren't common here).
- Unusual egg: both ends round, or both pointed, or yolk outside → non-kosher bird. One round + one pointed end, yolk inside → may rely on a knowledgeable person.
- Eggs of a terefa or nevela hen: forbidden (don't hatch them; a chick that hatched is kosher).
- Eggs found inside a kosher-slaughtered hen: with white and yolk → pareve; yolk only → like meat.

## 2. Blood in fertilized eggs

- Blood where the **chick starts to form** forbids the whole egg (Torah: Rashba, Rosh; rabbinic: Rif, Rambam).
- Blood elsewhere (a torn vessel) → remove it for appearances; egg permitted.
- Where does formation start? Yolk / white / the chalaza (Rashi, Tosafot). Custom (Rema): **any blood forbids the whole egg**.
- Hard-boiled or sucked eggs: no check (majority). Opened for an omelet or baking → look; check each in a separate bowl.

## 3. Unfertilized eggs

- No chick can form → blood isn't forbidden; remove it only for appearances (SA 66:7). Happens in ~1 in several thousand.
- In Israel, fertilized eggs may not be sold for food; 97%+ are supervised, unfertilized, **stamped**.
- So no need to check stamped eggs; many still check as a hiddur.

## 4. Honey

- Shimshon and Yonatan ate honey. But bees are non-kosher; why isn't it "from the impure"?
- (1) Bees only **store** nectar; it becomes honey in the hive. (2) R. Yaakov: a special derivation from the verse (Bekhorot 7b).
- Strain out dead bees and parts. Any taste they gave is a spoiled taste that doesn't forbid.

## 5. Royal jelly

- A secretion from glands in the bee's head; it turns a larva into a queen. Some see medical value.
- Strict: made inside the bee's body → forbidden; the Torah permitted only honey.
- Lenient: everything bees make comes from nectar (honey too needs their enzymes).
- Practice: for medical use it's fine mixed in honey (40×, batel) or in tasteless capsules; one who eats it plain has whom to rely on.
- **Propolis** and **pollen**: permitted like honey.

> Line to remember: honey is kosher because the bee is only the storehouse, not the source.
