## Cheat sheet (the big picture)

| Mixture | Nullified in |
|---|---|
| Different kind, liquid (min be-she-eino mino, laḥ be-laḥ) | 60 |
| Different kind, dry pieces | 60 (rabbinic) |
| Same kind, liquid | 60 (rabbinic) |
| Same kind, dry pieces | **Majority** (eat one at a time) |
| Teruma / orla, kilei ha-kerem | 100 / 200 |
| Beriya, ḥatikha ha-re'uya le-hitkabed, davar she-be-minyan, ḥametz, idolatry; davar she-yesh lo matirin in its kind | Never (not even 1000) |

## 1. The basic principles

- **Bittul be-shishim**: 60× and the taste is gone (even onion doesn't flavor beyond 60).
- **Bittul be-rov**: no taste given → majority rules ("aḥarei rabim lehatot").
- Taste transfers only if **different kind** (min be-she-eino mino) **and** **liquid/blended** (laḥ be-laḥ).
- Sages: if either condition holds, need 60 (a fence, and uniform measures), even for rabbinic prohibitions.

## 2. The four types

- Laḥ be-laḥ: tastes blend (cooked together; flour in flour). Yavesh be-yavesh: you don't know which piece, but they don't blend (even separate cups of gravy).
- Types 1–3 need 60; type 4 (same kind, dry) is batel by majority (most rishonim).
- Same kind, dry, but severe: teruma in 100, orla/kilei ha-kerem in 200.

## 3. Ta'am ke-ikkar; 60

- Noticeable forbidden taste → all forbidden (ta'am ke-ikkar). Spices need far more.
- Milk in meat soup: 60× → kosher. Everything in the pot counts (meat, vegetables, water, even bones).
- Treif meat in vegetable soup with 60×: soup kosher, but **remove the meat** (only its taste is batel).

## 4. A gentile taster

- Different kind: a gentile can taste; no taste → permitted even without 60 (SA, per Rambam, Tosafot, Rosh).
- Only if speaking innocently (doesn't know it matters), or a professional cook told the stakes.

## 5. Practice: 60

- Taste is slippery (people differ; "taste" vs. "changed taste") → custom everywhere: rely on **60**, not tasters (Rema).
- In great need, a reliable gentile's clear answer can permit.

## 6. Spices and setting agents

- A forbidden **spice** forbids while its taste is felt, even in 1000×.
- A forbidden agent that **sets or changes** the food (nevela rennet, forbidden yeast, vinegar/yeast that ferments a drink) isn't batel even in 60.
- Only with a permitted agent's help → "zeh ve-zeh gorem" → batel in 60.

## 7. Spoiled taste

- **Notein ta'am li-fgam**: if the forbidden item makes the dish worse, it's batel by majority, even if tasty itself.
- Neither better nor worse but noticeable → forbidden.
- Adds volume worth more than the taste loss: Rashba permits, Ran forbids; be strict, lenient in great need.
- Must spoil throughout; improved then spoiled (or reverse) → forbidden.

## 8. Same kind, dry

- One treif piece in two kosher → batel (5 in 6 too).
- Rabbinically: don't eat them all at once or cook together; eat one at a time (Rashba). Semag: two different people. Lekhatḥila be strict.

## 9. Not nullified in 60

- Never (even 1000): **beriya**, **ḥatikha ha-re'uya le-hitkabed**, **davar she-be-minyan**; also **ḥametz on Pesaḥ** and idolatry.
- Spices/setting agents: while effect is felt.
- Not batel in their own kind: **davar she-yesh lo matirin**, gentile wine.
- **Teruma/ḥalla** (death at the hands of Heaven): 100 (terumat ma'aser is 1/100). **Orla/kilei ha-kerem** (benefit forbidden): 200.

## 10. Beriya

- A whole creature (a worm) isn't batel even in 1000; its taste is batel in 60.
- Four conditions: (1) animal, not plant; (2) whole (not cut or crushed); (3) a limb with its own name lost if divided (gid ha-nasheh, ever min ha-ḥai; not ḥelev); (4) forbidden in itself (non-kosher bird, insect), not a kosher bird that died unslaughtered.

## 11. Important items

- Items always counted by unit because they're important (Badan pomegranates, Perekh nuts, big cabbages, sealed wine barrels; today a very expensive bottle).
- Not items counted for pricing (rice packs, cans, ordinary wine bottles).
- If one item of the mixture is lost (fell into the sea), assume it was the forbidden one → batel; eat two at a time.

## 12. A respectable piece

- A piece you'd serve an honored guest as the main dish: today at least a **quarter of a large chicken** or similar beef. In doubt → lenient (rabbinic).
- Some: only if ready to serve (cooked, not too big); may rely in need.
- Only if forbidden in itself (treif, meat cooked with milk), not just from absorbed taste. Cut smaller → back to normal rules.

## 13. Don't nullify on purpose

- No adding kosher food to reach 60 or a majority; no cutting important pieces to remove their status.
- Did it by mistake → batel. On purpose → forbidden to him and those it was for.
- Rabbinic prohibition: may add to nullify (Rambam, SA); Rosh, Rema strict; in need lenient.
- Grinding wheat with hard-to-remove insects is OK (purpose is flour).

## 14. Davar she-yesh lo matirin

- Something that will become permitted (ḥadash until the Omer; ma'aser sheni by redemption; an egg laid on Yom Tov) isn't batel in its own kind, even 1000: just wait.
- Mixed in a different kind with 60 → batel.
- Will spoil by then → not this category. A treif vessel mixed with kosher ones: wait 24 hours (some: kasher if easy).

## 15. Ḥanan in meat and milk

- **Ḥatikha na'aseit nevela**: meat cooked with milk becomes entirely forbidden.
- 10 ml milk in 90 ml meat → 100 ml "nevela" → in 5 L (only 50×) → all forbidden → into 250 L... A tiny drop can forbid millions of times its size. With 60 the first time, all would be fine.
- Moral: keep away from evil you can't nullify, or it spreads; but only briefly, since it spoils over time.

## 16. Ḥanan in other prohibitions

- Blood in kosher meat: Rabbeinu Efraim, Rosh, SA: the piece is forbidden but not "nevela" → count only the blood for 60.
- Rabbeinu Tam, Ramban, Ran, **Rema**: ḥanan applies to all prohibitions.

## 17. Kamma kamma batel / ḥozer ve-ni'or

- A first forbidden drop was batel; another falls. Combined they'd be too much.
- If together they give noticeable taste → forbidden.
- Same kind: each batel separately (if known in between), as long as together they don't reach a Torah-level prohibition (half the soup); then all "reawaken."

## 18. Prohibitions nullify each other

- 59 ml kosher + 1 ml milk + 1 ml blood → kosher: each helps nullify the other (different tastes).
- Two with the same taste (milk + nevela fat) in vegetables → forbidden. In kosher fat (same taste as them) → kosher.

> Line to remember: a drop of evil you don't nullify early can spread to the whole world; one in sixty disappears.
