## Cheat sheet (the big picture)

| Item | How to kasher |
|---|---|
| **Pots, cutlery** | Clean, wait 24 hrs or add soap, immerse in rolling boil ~3 seconds |
| **Sink, counter** | Clean and dry, pour boiling water section by section (or steam) |
| **Gas stove grates** | Light libun (or hagala); burn all flames ~15 min |
| **Oven** | Clean, max heat ~½ hour; baking pans not kashered (in great loss: same) |
| **Frying pan** | Light libun: heat empty until paper scorches |
| **Microwave, dishwasher, fridge** | Clean; boil soapy water 10 min / hottest cycle / just wash |

## 1. Ke-bol'o kakh polto

- **Hadaḥa** (cold use) washes off surface residue. **Hagala** (cooking) releases what stuck in with liquid. **Libun** (fire) burns it out.
- Baking/roasting is hotter and dry (no liquid softening absorption), so boiling water isn't enough.

## 2. Levels of hagala

- **Kli rishon on the fire** (pot, stirring spoon) → hagala in a pot on the fire.
- **Kli rishon off the fire** → hagala in very hot water off the fire.
- **Iruy** (poured on) → pour boiling water from a kli rishon.
- **Kli sheni**: many say it doesn't absorb, but kasher it anyway.
- Below ~45°C, or a kli shelishi → just rinse.
- Knife cutting treif on the fire → libun; after removal → hagala. Cold treif soup left 24 hours (kavush): hagala lekhatḥila; metal/glass may be rinsed in need.
- Many kasher everything as kli rishon in case; not needed if the use is clear.

## 3. Mixed uses

- Strictest use vs. majority use: lekhatḥila strictest; in need (libun would ruin it) majority.
- Count only the **forbidden** uses: a fork used once for treif in a kli rishon → hagala.

## 4. Preparing; pots

- **Clean first**; hagala releases taste but doesn't clean. Not cleaned → clean and redo.
- Handles too (iruy suffices). Removable handles: remove and clean, or soap around them well. Crevices: lots of soap.
- Immerse the pot in a bigger one (the rim got splashes). No big pot: boil water in it and drop a hot stone/small boiling pot in so it overflows the rim.

## 5. Waiting 24 hours

- Custom: not ben yomo; otherwise good taste might go back in without 60×, and meat and dairy tastes might mix in the water.
- After 24 hours the taste is spoiled (notein ta'am li-fgam).
- Public kashering stations add **liquid soap** to the water; you can too and kasher right away.

## 6. Hagala in practice

- Any clean pot; water at a rolling boil (with soap, even a treif pot can boil the water).
- Hold ~**3 seconds**; if the boil stops, wait for bubbles. Too big: one half, then the other.
- Cutlery: in a basket, shaken; or drop in one by one. Cold rinse after is custom, not required.

## 7. Libun

- For fire-used vessels (skewers, baking pans), including electric elements.
- "Until its peel flakes" / "sparks fly". Most: ~350–400°C to burn everything. Some: the same heat as used.
- Custom strict, and a vessel damaged by kashering isn't kashered (people would go easy) → **baking pans aren't kashered**. Great loss: oven at max ~½ hour. Same for a "wonder pot" and Teflon.

## 8. Between hagala and libun

- Pot used for food that dried out → hagala (started with liquid; its main use).
- Frying pan: most rishonim hagala (oil adds moisture); Rashba: libun (oil runs out). Practice: **light libun** on the stove until paper scorches or a thread burns.

## 9. Swapped meat/dairy pans

- Clean meat pan used for dairy: no forbidden mix (heteira bala) → hagala or max oven heat ~½ hour.
- Switching meat to dairy: same. Many Ashkenazim avoid it except in need (confusion); others allow it but not often.

## 10. Kashering a treif kitchen

| Item | Method |
|---|---|
| Sink and counter | Clean, dry, kettle of boiling water, bottom then walls then outward; quality steamer works. Delicate counter: clean only |
| Gas stove | Clean; light libun (or hagala) on grates; clean the rest; burn flames ~15 min |
| Electric/ceramic/induction | Clean, max ~15 min (induction: pour boiling water, heat pots on it) |
| Cutlery, pots | Clean, boil with soap, immerse one by one |
| Oven | Clean, max ~½ hour; pans not kashered |
| Microwave | Clean, hagala the turntable, boil soapy water ~10 min at max |
| Dishwasher | Clean filter, hottest cycle |
| Table | Delicate: wash. Hot pots go on it: wash + pour boiling water |
| Fridge, cabinets | Soap and water |
| Mixer/blender | Cold use: wash. Hot use: hagala |

## 11. Using a treif kitchen or grill

- Lekhatḥila don't use un-kashered or wrong-type vessels even for cold food.
- In need (a rented cabin): clean treif or wrong-type vessels for **cold** food (salad plates, cold drinks, yogurt spoons, cereal bowls).
- A grill's grate touches food → **libun**, fire reaching every part; no need to clean first.

> Line to remember: kasher it the way it was used: water for what was cooked, fire for what was roasted.
