## Cheat sheet (the big picture)

| Topic | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|
| **Health** | A mitzva; danger is treated more strictly than prohibition; follow today's doctors |
| **Uncovered drinks** | No longer forbidden (no snakes); but no kiddush on wine left open for hours |
| **Hygiene** | Wash after raw meat/fish, refrigerate, reheat until steaming, no knife stuck in fruit |
| **Meat with fish** | Not dangerous today; custom: separate plates, cutlery, a drink between |
| **Fish with milk** | Permitted for all communities today |
| **Spirits and demons** | Not a concern today (pairs, peeled onions overnight, tekufa), unless your outlook includes them |

## 1. The mitzva of health

- "Guard yourself and your soul very carefully" (Devarim 4:9, 15): faith requires staying alive. Rambam: a healthy body is among God's ways.
- "**Ḥamira sakanta me-isura**": danger is stricter than prohibition, even remote risk.
- Unhealthy eating is also forbidden; caring for health fulfills "walk in His ways." If wasting food is forbidden, all the more damaging your body.
- Follow current medical consensus: cut processed food, sugar, margarine, salt, white flour, saturated/trans fat (soft drinks, snacks, cold cuts, bourekas, cakes); eat vegetables and fruit, drink water, don't overeat, exercise, sleep. But avoid extreme diets: joy and naturalness are part of health.

## 2. Uncovered liquids

- Snakes used to leave venom in drinks or peck fruit → uncovered drinks were forbidden (even terumah wine poured out).
- Rishonim: no snakes near homes → permitted (SA 116:1). Forbidden only if you saw a venomous creature drink from it.
- No kiddush on wine left open for hours (you wouldn't serve it to an honored guest). Some still cover drinks (honor to the decree, cleanliness).

## 3. Health warnings

- Don't put coins in your mouth; avoid human sweat in food; don't keep bread in your armpit.
- Cooks: wash hands with soap before food and after the bathroom; don't cook for others while contagious.
- After handling raw meat or fish: wash hands and cutting surfaces with soap.
- Refrigerate cooked food; reheat liquids until **steaming**; keep raw-egg foods (mayonnaise) cold (salmonella, E. coli).
- Don't stick a knife in fruit (someone may fall on it).

## 4. Meat and fish together

- Old medical view: causes tzara'at and bad breath (Pesaḥim 76b; SA 116:2). Rambam permitted fish roasted with meat; most later authorities stayed cautious.
- Today medicine is systematic and worldwide; all agree it's harmless. The Sages' rule was medical guidance, not a halakhic decree → no prohibition.
- But it's a hallmark of the Jewish kitchen: don't cook together or serve on one plate; separate plate, knife, fork (or wipe with napkin/bread), clean fingers, drink something between.

## 5. Fish with milk

- Permitted (Mishna, Gemara): fish look different and need no sheḥita.
- Beit Yosef warned of danger → many in the East and North Africa avoided it (some only milk, not butter); other Sephardi poskim permitted.
- Today all may eat it: the concern was medical (maybe local bacteria), and with pasteurized milk and medical consensus it's gone.

## 6. Metaphysical dangers

- Talmud: avoid drinking in **pairs** (demons, perhaps from Zoroastrian dualism). "Whoever is particular, they are particular with him; whoever isn't, they aren't. But one should be cautious."
- Explanation: the spiritual world has many dimensions; in imaginative ones, forces appear as spirits and demons; in rational ones, as ideas. You're affected by the world your consciousness lives in.
- Today almost everyone lives in a rational spiritual consciousness → no need to worry, unless your upbringing or nature includes such beliefs.

## 7. For those who are concerned

- **Peeled garlic, onion, egg left overnight**: some avoid; many don't (not in Rambam, most rishonim or SA). Families that keep it may discard without bal tashḥit.
- Water at the **tekufa** (season change): some avoid unless iron is in it; R. Hai Gaon and Ibn Ezra: no concern; that's the practice.
- Don't drink from rivers/lakes at night (you might swallow a leech), a real concern; not the spirits.

## 8. Warnings with other reasons

- Don't throw **bread crumbs** on the floor: brings poverty (the angel of livelihood is "cleanliness," of poverty "filth"). Fits the moral lesson: kicking God's blessing; neglect spreads to finances.
- Don't store food under the **bed** (dirt, forgotten, mold; sleep is 1/60 of death). If it was, you may still eat it.

> Line to remember: guarding your health is guarding the one who serves God.
