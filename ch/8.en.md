## Cheat sheet (the big picture)

| Berakha | Essential? | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **Ha-gafen** | ✓ essential | Wine; else alcoholic ḥamar medina (beer) |
| **Besamim** | Added | Comfort for the departing neshama yeteira; not after Yom Tov / Yom Kippur |
| **Me'orei ha-esh** | Added | Adam's fire; braided candle, open flame, not electric |
| **Ha-mavdil** | ✓ essential | Distinguishing sacred from profane |

**Also:** before havdala: no melakha until Ata Ḥonantanu / "barukh ha-mavdil"; no eating or drinking (water ok) until havdala over wine · missed it → until end of Tuesday (only gafen + mavdil).

## 1. The basis of havdala

- Mirror of kiddush: in **prayer** (Ata Ḥonantanu in the 4th berakha, the berakha of da'at) and over **wine**.
- History: first only in prayer (poverty), then only over wine, then both.
- Forgot Ata Ḥonantanu → don't repeat; havdala over wine covers it.
- Torah or rabbinic? Rambam: part of Zakhor (Torah); Rosh: rabbinic.
- **Women** obligated (tied to kiddush), but some exempt → le-khatchila hear from a man. No man, or he already made havdala → she makes it herself, all four berakhot.

## 2. The order

- Opening verses for a good omen, then **Ha-gafen → besamim → ha-esh → ha-mavdil**.
- No besamim or candle → make havdala without; say those berakhot later that night if available.
- Order goes from the most physical sense to the most refined: **taste → smell → sight → discernment**.

> Line to remember: havdala climbs the senses, from tasting to knowing the difference between holy and ordinary.

## 3. Customs

- Cup in the **right** hand; for besamim switch (rest the cup or hold it in the left), back to the right for Ha-mavdil.
- **Sit** (SA: defines the group) or **stand** (Rema: honor) — if standing, gather around.
- Clean cup, revi'it; custom: fill until it **overflows a bit** (blessing), unlike kiddush.
- Ideally drink a whole revi'it (for al ha-gefen); melo lugmav is enough.
- Listeners silent until he drinks.

## 4. Ḥamar medina

- Wine is best: "havdala on wine → sons" (Shevuot 18b).
- No wine → **ḥamar medina**, e.g. beer (She-hakol). Have wine but prefer beer → better wine; beer is allowed.
- Vodka/arak ok if you can drink a melo lugmav.
- Non-alcoholic (coffee, tea, fresh juice): machloket. Practice: **alcoholic only**; be-di'eved dignified non-alcoholic. Not soft drinks.

## 5. Besamim

- To revive the soul sad at losing the **neshama yeteira**.
- Not after Yom Tov; not when Yom Tov starts on Motza'ei Shabbat; not after Yom Kippur.
- Everyone should smell before he goes on; can't smell → no berakha.
- Ashkenazim: always "**borei minei vesamim**." Sephardim: the specific berakha (myrtle/rosemary → atzei vesamim).
- No berakha on air fresheners/deodorants. Synthetic: may say minei vesamim. **Etrog with cloves** → minei vesamim.

## 6. The candle

- Recalls Adam striking two stones on the first Motza'ei Shabbat.
- Ideally an **avuka** (2+ wicks, or two matches); be-di'eved one wick.
- Bright enough to tell coins apart → look at palm lines and fingernails.
- Listeners must benefit from the light; heard but didn't see → yotzei havdala, light later and say ha-esh.
- Mehadrin: turn off the electric lights.
- Not over a **yahrzeit** or honor candle; not over an **electric bulb** (not open fire).

## 7. Until when

- Missed havdala (soldier, forgot, even on purpose) → until end of **Tuesday** (most Rishonim; SA and Rema 299:6), even after eating.
- Geonim: Sunday only; Behag: only if he hasn't eaten. Some follow this.
- Later days: only **Ha-gafen + Ha-mavdil**; besamim and fire are only Motza'ei Shabbat.

## 8. Before havdala

- No melakha until a few minutes after tzeit; then after **Ata Ḥonantanu** or "barukh ha-mavdil bein kodesh le-ḥol" (even rabbinic melakhot).
- No **eating or drinking** after shki'a until havdala over wine; water ok (Kaf Ha-ḥayim: not even water).
- Zimun over wine at se'uda shlishit: SA → leader drinks (continuation of the meal); MB → save it for havdala. Sheva berakhot → drink.
- Havdala over wine before Ma'ariv is fine (then say Ata Ḥonantanu); don't drink a full revi'it before davening.
