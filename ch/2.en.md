## Cheat sheet (the big picture)

| Topic | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|
| **When to prepare** | Mainly Friday morning; finish by midday if you can and rest/learn after |
| **Food** | Better than weekday food; borrow if you can repay, otherwise keep it simple |
| **Clothes, body, home** | Special clothes, hot shower, haircut/nails, clean house and set table |
| **Do something yourself** | Even great rabbis chopped wood, salted fish, cleaned |
| **No big meal Friday** | Brit / pidyon ha-ben ok, preferably before midday; no bread meal ~3 hours before Shabbat |
| **No regular work Friday afternoon** | From Minḥa ketana; ad hoc work and Shabbat needs are fine |
| **Things that run into Shabbat** | Fine if set up before (timers, cooking, sprinklers) |
| **Boats / planes / trains** | No pleasure sailing from Wednesday; no plane/train that travels on Shabbat |

## 1. Preparing all week

- **Shammai**: found a fine animal → "for Shabbat"; found a better one → ate the first. **Hillel**: "Blessed is God day by day," trusting better would come for Shabbat.
- Most people should follow **Shammai** (MB 250:2). Today: plan shopping so Shabbat food is the best.
- Say "this is **l'kavod Shabbat kodesh**" when shopping (Zakhor). Good to **taste** the food on Friday.

## 2. Friday

- Main preparation is Friday **morning** ("on the sixth day they shall prepare"). Nice custom: bake challah and separate ḥalla.
- Don't shop before **Shaḥarit** (unless food will run out). Keep your regular learning, then shop.
- Friday shopping is better than Thursday; but time-consuming or scarce items → Thursday.
- Cooking Thursday to enter Shabbat **calm** beats cooking Friday and arriving exhausted.
- Avoid Friday fights. R. Meir made peace for a couple three Fridays in a row (Gittin 52a).
- Holy custom: finish by **midday**, then rest and learn.

## 3. Buying food for Shabbat

- Oneg Shabbat brings a limitless portion, saves from the "birth pangs of Mashiaḥ," and brings wealth (Shabbat 118a–119a).
- Spend more than on weekdays (suggested: about **double**). Not required: the most expensive.
- Short on money → save during the week for something special (small fish).
- Money for Shabbat, Yom Tov and kids' Torah isn't counted in your yearly allotment (Taz).
- **Borrow** for Shabbat ("borrow on Me and I'll repay," Beitza 15b) only if you can realistically repay.
- Can't repay → "make your Shabbat like a weekday and don't depend on others" (Pesaḥim 112a). Already on tzedaka → may take for Shabbat too.

## 4. Clothes

- "Honor it": Shabbat clothes different from weekday clothes (Shabbat 113a). Best: nothing worn during the week (Arizal); clean undergarments.
- Even **alone** → dress up; it's for Shabbat, not for people.
- Only one garment, or away unexpectedly → clean and spruce it up (R. Simlai). Soldiers → dress uniform if possible.
- Ezra's takana: laundry on **Thursday**. With machines, Friday is ok; Thursday still better.

## 5. Body and home

- Mitzva to wash in **hot** water before Shabbat (Shabbat 25b); today, the whole body.
- Don't shower too close to Shabbat (risk of turning off lights/boiler).
- Haircut, shave, nails Friday (preferably after midday); busy → Thursday.
- Clean house, nice tablecloth and dishes; keep it nice all Shabbat.
- Two angels on Friday night: candles lit, table set, bed made → the bad angel must say **Amen** (Shabbat 119b).
- Even if everything went wrong → **no fighting**. "Better a meal of greens with love…" (Mishlei 15:17).

> Line to remember: Shabbat shows holiness can live in food, clothes and a clean house, not only in prayer.

## 6. Doing it yourself

- Even an important person with help must do **something** himself (SA 250:1).
- Rava salted fish, R. Ḥisda chopped vegetables, Rabba and R. Yosef chopped wood, R. Naḥman cleaned (Shabbat 119a). These were heads of the generation.
- Three things before dark: tithed? eruv? light the candles! Today: check **timers**, fridge light/Shabbat mode, food on the plata, open bottles and cans, separate attached yogurts.

## 7. No big meal on Friday

- No unusually large meal: ruins appetite, makes Shabbat look ordinary, gets in the way of preparation (SA 249:2).
- Even a **siyum** → not a big one; a regular meal before midday is ok.
- **Brit** or **pidyon ha-ben** on their day → ok, preferably before midday.
- Regular meal ok, but avoid bread in the last **3 hours**. Snack ok if it doesn't spoil the appetite.
- Fasting Friday was a ḥasidut; if hard, don't.

## 8. No work on Friday afternoon

| From | Ruling |
|---|---|
| **Minḥa ketana** (2.5 seasonal hours before sunset) | Main view; may rely on it (SA 251:1) |
| Minḥa gedola (½ hour after midday) | Stricter view |

- Forbidden: **regular** work (carpentry, tailoring, computer work, writing a sefer Torah, paid editing).
- Allowed: **ad hoc** work (watering, laundry machine, sewing a button, writing a dvar Torah). Not for pay every week.
- Work clearly **for Shabbat** → even for pay (barber, driver, electrician for Shabbat needs). Tailor for pay → no (not obviously for Shabbat); for free → yes.
- Also allowed: a poor person needing Shabbat money; preventing a **loss**; whatever is allowed on Ḥol Ha-mo'ed.
- Stores: close at least ½ hour before; custom is earlier. Travelers: arrive ½ hour early; R. Mordechai Eliyahu: allow **double** the travel time.

## 9. Things that run into Shabbat

- Beit Hillel: no obligation to "rest" your tools (SA 252:1). → Start before, let it run.
- Cooking on the plata (don't touch until cooked), **timers**, **sprinklers**, factory machines → ok.
- **Noisy** machines: SA permits; Rema prefers not, unless there's a loss or great need.

## 10. Boat trips into Shabbat

- Don't put yourself where you'll have to desecrate Shabbat. From **Wednesday**, no setting sail for pleasure (Shabbat 19a).
- Reasons: might have to help steer; benefit from non-Jew's work; teḥum (shallow water); seasickness ruins oneg.
- None apply (big ship, deep water, non-Jewish crew and majority) → ok even Friday.
- Sun–Tue → ok even if a problem might arise.

## 11. Sailing for a mitzva; Jewish-owned boats

- For a **mitzva** on a non-Jewish boat → ok even Friday (most: even if he won't anchor on Shabbat).
- Not setting sail **on Shabbat** itself; can board before Shabbat and stay on.
- Boat owned by Jews who desecrate Shabbat → in practice **not**, even early in the week.

## 12. Planes and trains on Friday

- No boarding a plane/train that will travel on Shabbat, even with a non-Jewish pilot/driver.
- Reasons: **teḥum** (2,000 amot; 12 mil possibly Torah); no **oneg**; like the wagon decree; it's **uvdin de-ḥol**.
