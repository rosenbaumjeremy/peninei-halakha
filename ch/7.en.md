## Cheat sheet (the big picture)

| Meal | When | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Friday night** | After kiddush | Bread, ideally a bit more than a keveitza |
| **2. Day** (most important) | Before midday | Bread; save the best food for it |
| **3. Se'uda shlishit** | ½ hour after midday → start before shki'a | Bread; be-di'eved mezonot → meat/fish → fruit |
| **Melaveh malka** | Motza'ei Shabbat, ideally within 4 hrs / before midnight | Kezayit of bread + something special |

**Also:** leḥem mishneh at all three meals · no fasting on Shabbat · forgot Retzei: before Ha-tov ve-ha-metiv → short berakha; after → repeat (not at se'uda shlishit).

## 1. The mitzva of the meals

- Three meals save from the birth pangs of Mashiaḥ, Gehinnom and the war of Gog u-Magog (Shabbat 118a). Oneg → "everything his heart desires," "boundless inheritance" (Yeshayahu 58:14).
- Delight in **Shabbat**, not in the stomach: the meal should leave you wanting more Torah.
- Shabbat = **oneg** (inner, refined); Yom Tov = **simḥa** (meat and wine). Maybe that's why **fish** is a Shabbat food.

## 2. Parameters

- Two mitzvot: **oneg** (meals, snacks, nap) and **three meals** (Shabbat 117b).
- Third meal too early (before ½ hour after midday) → not yotzei (SA 291:2). Missed Friday night → three by day; missed the morning → two after midday.
- Bread + tasty food: meat, wine, fruit (SA 250:2); don't like meat → what you enjoy. Fish at every meal (kabbala), not required.
- Eating a lot is a mitzva on Shabbat; **overeating** to the point of sleeping and no Torah isn't oneg.
- No fasting on Shabbat even an hour; not eating before midday = transgressing (SA 288:1).
- Sick / no appetite: a bit more than a keveitza, or a kezayit; if even that hurts → don't eat.

## 3. Leḥem mishneh

- Two loaves = the double manna; Shabbat is "double" (Zakhor/Shamor, two lambs).
- Hold **both** during the berakha, cut one (SA 274:1); Shlah/Gra cut both (use small ones).
- Which to cut: bottom (SA, draw it closer), top (Arizal), bottom at night / top by day (Rema). Arizal: 12 small loaves.
- Must be **whole**: don't peel the sticker first. Frozen ok; two whole sliced loaves in packages be-di'eved; else two slices.
- Se'uda shlishit also two loaves; if only one, one (in the desert that's what was left).

## 4. The day meal is the main one

- **Kiddush**: night is more important. **Kavod**: the day meal is (Pesaḥim 105b; SA 271:3).
- Some skip fish Friday night so the night meal doesn't outshine the day.
- Others: hot fresh foods (fish, soup) at night are fine; for things that could go either way (wine, fruit), give the day priority. Cholent and kugel solve it for most.
- Ideally bread right after kiddush; snacks aren't forbidden, just don't kill the appetite.
- Light dairy lunch and main meat meal at se'uda shlishit → also fine (it's still by day).

## 5. Se'uda shlishit

- Be-di'eved: **mezonot** → meat/fish → (cooked) fruit (SA 291:5).
- Plan to be hungry: eat less at lunch if they're close. Full → a bit more than a keveitza, be-di'eved a kezayit.
- Wine: no kiddush at se'uda shlishit (SA 291:4), but nice to have wine.
- Start before **shki'a** (be-di'eved until **13.5 min** after); with bread you can continue past tzeit. Without bread → stop eating and drinking at shki'a.
- Sheva berakhot at se'uda shlishit: the leader, ḥatan and kalla drink even after tzeit.

## 6. Birkat ha-mazon

- **Retzei** inside the 3rd berakha (a request; ends with Yerushalayim).
- Forgot: before starting Ha-tov ve-ha-metiv → "…asher natan Shabbatot li-menuḥa…mekadesh ha-Shabbat"; already started → **repeat bentching** (SA 188:6).
- At **se'uda shlishit** → don't repeat (bread not obligatory). Same for Ya'aleh ve-yavo on Rosh Ḥodesh.
- Started before shki'a, finished after tzeit → still Retzei. Rosh Ḥodesh on Motza'ei Shabbat → avoid bread after tzeit.

## 7. Meaning of melaveh malka

- Escort the departing queen like a beloved guest (Shabbat 119b).
- Vi-yhi No'am extends Shabbat's spiritual light; melaveh malka extends it to our eating all week (Arizal).
- The **luz** bone, source of the resurrection, is nourished only by melaveh malka.
- R. Abahu's calf: make a **special** dish, not just leftovers (the lion story).

> Line to remember: you don't drop a beloved guest at the door; you walk the queen a little way.

## 8. Laws of melaveh malka

- Set table, tablecloth, stay in Shabbat clothes. Men and women alike.
- Ideally bread + a special dish; not hungry → kezayit of bread; or mezonot / cooked food / fruit.
- Limited food → the 3 Shabbat meals come first.
- Soon after Shabbat; else within **4 hours**, or before **midnight**, or any time that night.
- Se'uda shlishit ran past Shabbat → custom: still eat melaveh malka.
