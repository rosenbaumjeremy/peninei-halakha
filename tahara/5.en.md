## Cheat sheet (the big picture)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| **The tevila** | Whole body and every hair under at once; one finger or hair out → invalid, immerse again fully |
| **Ḥatzitza (Torah)** | On **most** of the body/hair **and** she minds it |
| **Ḥatzitza (Sages)** | **Either** condition → invalid. Things she *wants* on her (good makeup, intact nail polish, rings she never removes) → part of the body, not a ḥatzitza |
| **Hidden places** | Mouth, armpits, folds: water needn't reach, but no ḥatzitza there. Throat, rectum, inner ear → neither |
| **Preparation (Ezra)** | Wash and **comb** hair (warm water); wash the whole body, esp. folds; clean ears, nose, teeth; nails clean or cut |
| **Position** | Knees bent, leaning forward, legs a bit apart, arms a bit out; nothing clenched. Two immersions is good |
| **Berakha** | "*…al ha-tevila*": before (some Mizraḥi) or between two immersions (Ashkenazim, most Mizraḥi) |
| **When** | Night after day 7, after tzeit. Need → bein ha-shmashot; great need → just before sunset (home after tzeit); else day 8 |
| **Don't delay** | Not even one night, not even in pregnancy, not even after a fight |

## 1. Tahara through tevila

- The whole body in the water: "*ve-raḥatz ba-mayim et kol besaro*" (Vayikra 15:16); "*et*" → including all hair (Eruvin 4b).
- One finger or one hair out → **invalid**; re-immersing that part doesn't help, only the whole body again.
- However many showers and baths, without a mikveh she stays tamei; closeness is a Torah issur (Rambam, Issurei Bi'a 4:3; SA YD 197:1).
- Idea: immersed in ḥesed and in Knesset Yisrael, she's reborn. Partial vs. complete is like a number vs. infinity.
- And **no ḥatzitza** (glue, dough): all her traits and wants must connect to the Source without barrier. So: no clothing and nothing in between.

## 2. Ḥatzitza

- **Torah:** blocks only if (1) on **most** of the body or hair, **and** (2) she **minds** it (wants it off).
- **Sages:** **either one** blocks. → glue, honey, dirt, hard mud, eye crust, even small → ḥatzitza (Mikva'ot 9:2; SA YD 198:7, 12–16).
- On a small area, and neither she nor most women mind it → part of the body → no ḥatzitza. **Eye makeup, nail polish, nail extensions** in good shape → women want them → no ḥatzitza; chipped to where they'd be removed → ḥatzitza (198:1, 8, 17).
- Something she **sometimes** wants off → ḥatzitza: tight clothing or jewelry the water can't get under. Loose → water gets under → fine (198:1–2, 23).
    - **Tight ring** she never removes → fine; an ornate one she takes off for kneading → ḥatzitza. Loose ring → fine.
    - **Earrings/piercings**: never removed → fine; sometimes → ḥatzitza.
- **Le-khatchila custom:** righteous women remove **all clothing, jewelry and makeup** (Rema 198:1). Only with some need, follow the basic law:
    - Tevila on Friday night and wants makeup → long-lasting makeup.
    - Must immerse in the **sea** and afraid to undress → a loose garment; a tight swimsuit → once in the water, lift each part so water gets under; then it joins the sea water.

## 3. Hidden places and folds

- Water must touch the **visible** body ("*besaro*", Kiddushin 25a).
- **Beit ha-setarim / beit ha-kematim** (sometimes visible, sometimes hidden): water needn't reach, but **no ḥatzitza** there (Mikva'ot 8:5; SA 198:24–25).
    - Mouth (closed vs. open), nose (standing vs. face up), armpits and body folds.
- **Three levels:**
    1. Visible → water must touch; ḥatzitza invalidates.
    2. Hidden places/folds → water needn't touch; ḥatzitza invalidates.
    3. **"Swallowed" places** (throat, rectum, inner ear and nose canals) → neither; no need to clean.

## 4. Ezra's enactment: ḥafifa and preparation

- Torah: look over the body and feel the hair. **Ezra** enacted **ḥafifa** (washing the hair) and **combing** to undo knots (BK 82a).
    - **Warm water** le-khatchila (cold hardens oils and tangles) (SA/Rema 199:2).
- Wash places where dirt collects: armpits, between legs and toes, navel, folds; clean nose, ears, mouth; **brush teeth** (Nidda 66b). In short: wash the whole body well in warm water (SA 199:1).
- **Nails:** usually cut → cut before tevila. Growing them → clean well (dirt under them blocks); polish must be intact (§12).
- Removing hair women usually remove for beauty → not a ḥatzitza issue, but adorning herself is part of preparing for ona (*Simḥat Ha-bayit* 2:5).
- Immersed **without** checking her body or washing/combing hair → **invalid**, even if checked afterward (maybe it was there and fell off) (SA 199:8). Checked and combed but didn't wash the body → valid unless a ḥatzitza is known.

## 5. How to immerse

- Le-khatchila: **knees bent, leaning forward, legs slightly apart, arms slightly away**: the most folds open. Not fully upright (folds under the chest, buttocks) or bent too far (stomach folds).
    - Some invalidate upright or deeply bent. **Practice:** any **natural** position → valid (SA 198:35).
    - **Clenched** (fist, lips pressed, arms/legs pressed to the body) → not natural → **invalid** where water didn't reach (Mikva'ot 8:5; SA 198:27, 38–39).
    - If those parts were **already wet** from the mikveh before clenching → valid even per the strict (Rambam; Taz).
- Feet don't need to leave the floor (wet first, the water connects) (198:30).
- So **two immersions** is good: the second surely reaches everything.

## 6. The berakha

- "*Barukh… asher kideshanu be-mitzvotav ve-tzivanu **al ha-tevila***."
- Forbidden because of a universally accepted stringency (e.g., the women's 7 days) → still a berakha. Tahor by halakha, immersing only to satisfy all views → **no** berakha.
- **When:**
    - **Before** (like all mitzvot), dressed in a robe or wrapped in a towel, then go in (Rambam; SA YD 200:1). Some Mizraḥi and North African women.
    - **After**, like a convert (who isn't Jewish before), and better to say it tahor. In practice: **immerse, berakha, immerse again** → both views. All Ashkenazim and most Mizraḥi/North African. Follow her mother.
- Berakha in the water: many fold their arms below or over the heart (separating heart from the lower body). Head covering isn't required; some cover it (the balanit places a towel edge).

## 7. Ḥafifa and tevila customs

- **When to wash:** many say at night close to tevila (Rif, Rambam, Ra'avad); some say by day so she won't rush (Rashi, Rosh); some start by day and finish at night (SA 199:3). **In practice:** whatever lets her do it **calmly and thoroughly**. At home first → comb a bit again before tevila.
- Know how long a calm, thorough preparation takes you, and set aside that much time.
- **Water height:** about heart level for most women (198:36). Deeper or shallower is fine if everything goes under.
- **Warm water** le-khatchila (pleasant; relaxed body, nothing clenched). Cold is fine too, as for millennia.
- ✗ Standing on something that can become tamei (chair, stool) → **invalid** (198:31). ✗ Le-khatchila on something she's afraid of falling off (clenches). Be-di'avad, if she immersed properly → valid.

## 8. The balanit

- She can't see herself, so a responsible woman watches that **every hair** went under (198:40). From this, a balanit who also helps check hidden spots and guides new women.
- **Correct approach:** while still dressed, ask the woman **what help she wants** and don't impose; unwanted interference hurts. Don't impose **stringencies**: removing good makeup or insubstantial color, cutting well-kept nails, hard skin (§12–13).
- No woman available: **short hair** → alone. **Long** → her husband watches (Maharam; Noda Bi-yehuda), or she loosely wraps her hair in cloth or a ribbon (198:40).

## 9. When to immerse

- **After tzeit** at the end of day 7 (started Monday night → immerses Monday night a week later).
- **When needed** → bein ha-shmashot.
- **Sha'at ha-dechak** → shortly **before sunset**, provided she doesn't get home before tzeit.
- Can't even then (personal reasons, criminals nearby, dangerous cold) → **day 8 in daylight** (Nidda 67b; SA 197:3–4). Keep it discreet (so others won't learn it's fine without need), and don't be together before tzeit.

## 10. Don't delay

- Mitzva to start the 7 days **as soon as possible** and immerse right after: ona and peru u-revu. Without ones, ✗ delaying even **one night** (SA 197:2).
- Even when pregnancy isn't possible (pregnant, nursing) → ona.
- **After a fight** → certainly don't delay; as long as they're not divorcing, immerse and make up, and be together in love (Beit Yosef).
- Illness prevents intimacy → still immerse; other affection is a mitzva too (Maharam Lublin; Shakh 197:3).
- Discretion is good, but **not** a reason to delay. As **guests** and she can't hide it → don't delay without both agreeing.

## 11. Tevila on Friday night

- Do ḥafifa, washing and preparation on **Friday**, whenever she can do it calmly; then gather the hair so no knots form. If done in the morning, wash and comb a bit again close to Shabbat.
- **Warm mikveh** on Shabbat: most communities rely on the view that the hot-water bath issur doesn't apply to a mitzva tevila. Others: lukewarm, or at bein ha-shmashot (*Shabbat* 14:9).
- ✗ Wringing the hair to dry it. ✓ Towel-drying (squeezing is incidental and unwanted) (*Shabbat* 14:8).
- **Up to 2 days** between ḥafifa and tevila when needed: Yom Tov on Friday → wash Thursday; tie hair loosely; Friday night rinse spots, clean teeth, check everything, immerse. Rosh Ha-shana Thursday–Friday → wash Wednesday (Nidda 67b–68a; SA 199:6).
- Some say a woman who **put off** an earlier tevila shouldn't immerse Friday night. Le-khatchila don't push it there; if she did, immerse Friday night, don't delay another night.

## 12. Makeup, nail polish, jewelry

- Ḥatzitza = attached and she **minds** it (e.g., embarrassed to go out with it). What she **wants** on her is part of the body.
- **Makeup** (eyes, face, foundation, lipstick) → not a ḥatzitza **if done well and stays intact** through tevila. Smudged to where she/most women would fix it → ḥatzitza.
- **Polish, gel, extensions** intact → fine; chipped enough that she/most would redo them → ḥatzitza.
- **Rings, earrings, piercings** never removed → fine (Tashbetz 3:280). Removed sometimes (ornate ring for cooking, earrings for washing) → ḥatzitza.
- **Insubstantial color** (ink, henna, hair dye), **hand/body cream** even if greasy → no substance → fine. Hiddur: immerse without cream and remove such color when possible.
- **Substantial paint** (you can feel it) → ḥatzitza. A **painter** by trade → stains she doesn't mind aren't (198:17); still, remove if easy.

## 13. Nails and attached skin

- **Long nails** are part of the body → fine; dirt under them that she'd remove → ḥatzitza. So the custom to cut; one who wants long nails just keeps them clean.
- **Wart** (even loosening), **hangnails, peeling sunburned skin, hard dry skin** on the foot → not a ḥatzitza. Hiddur: remove if easy (Rema 198:22).

## 14. Scabs, bandages, casts

- **Scab/dried pus** she'd leave (painful, or helps healing) → part of the body. One she'd remove (scab beyond the wound) → ḥatzitza. A thorough warm wash removes what's usually removed anyway.
- **Bandage, Band-Aid, thick ointment** she won't remove (pain, healing) → fine; no pain or harm in removing → ḥatzitza.
- **Stitches**: while needed → fine; once removable and bothersome → ḥatzitza. Dissolving stitches → always fine.
- **Splinter** fully embedded → fine. Sticking out → she wants it out → ḥatzitza (198:11); tried and failed → now fine.
- **Cast** that's hard to remove (harm or great bother) → she wants it on → fine. Waterproof → immerse with it; otherwise wrap in plastic sealed to the skin and immerse. Some are strict, but: she needs it now; under most of the body it's only d'rabbanan; strictness hurts ona → rule **leniently**.

## 15. Hidden places: teeth and ears

- **Food between teeth** → ḥatzitza (hidden places must be free). So many didn't eat **meat** on tevila day (SA 198:24). With **floss**, one who cleans well needn't refrain.
- **Fillings** (permanent or temporary), **bonded braces**, **dentures** → fine.
- **Contact lenses** → not tight on the eye → fine; some say they are → le-khatchila remove.
- **Contraceptive ring** → fine. **Contraceptive patch** → remove; be-di'avad fine.
- **Ear condition** where the doctor forbids water → seal as he instructs and immerse. Prefer a God-fearing doctor so it's only as needed.

## 16. Hair

- Hair equals the body: ḥatzitza on **most** hair that she minds → Torah invalidation; on a minority → d'rabbanan. Glue/dirt she'd remove → ḥatzitza.
- **Knots:** two hairs together → loose, water enters → fine. A single hair knotted on itself or another → water doesn't enter; if enough that she or most women mind → ḥatzitza (198:5).
- **Lice/nits:** wash and comb as usual; what remains isn't a ḥatzitza (women can't help it).
- **Extensions** (hair or lashes) meant to stay → wanted → fine.
- **Braids**, thick or thin → loose → fine, just no tight elastic.
- **Dreadlocks** → ḥatzitza (hairs stuck together with knots and oil over most of the hair; most women mind). One who very much wants them for beauty may rely on the lenient and immerse with them.
