## Cheat sheet (the big picture)

| Situation | Onot perisha (when to refrain from intimacy) |
|---|---|
| **No fixed cycle** | Three onot from the **last** period: **haflaga** (same interval as last time), **beinonit** (day 30 = 4 weeks + 1 day), **ḥodesh** (same Hebrew date) |
| **Fixed cycle** (3 times in a row) | Only that one ona |
| **Fixed, then she saw off-schedule** | The fixed one + ḥodesh + haflaga from the new one (no beinonit) |
| **Semi-fixed** ("never shorter than 32") | Only haflaga |
| **Veset ha-guf** (a body sign before her period) | When she feels it, refrain to the end of that ona |
| **Confirmed pregnant / nursing** (no period yet, < 24 months) | None |
| **Hormonal pills suppressing the period** | None; after stopping, from 48 h after the last pill (until she learns her pattern) |
| **Menopause** (90 days without) | None |

**Ona** = a day (sunrise→sunset) or a night (sunset→sunrise), matching when the last period started. Refrain from **intimacy only** (hugging is ok unless it may lead further). At the end of the ona, an internal bedika.

## 1. Ḥezkat tahara

- After a proper tevila she's presumed **tahor**; intimacy is permitted with no bedika.
- Better **not** to check right before (he might worry she felt something), nor right after (don't cast doubt on the mitzva) (Nidda 12a).
- "*All women are presumed tahor for their husbands*," even returning from a trip (Nidda 15a). He needn't worry she missed something while he was away; he may come to her even if she's asleep.
- Except at the **ona of her expected period** (below).
- Unrelated to intimacy, checking often is praiseworthy (catches an unexpected period) (Nidda 13a). Today nearly all women do this by glancing at the panty liner or toilet paper.
    - One prone to **stains** (wounds, IUD, pills) → better **not** look at the first wipes; the point is catching a period, not finding stains.

## 2. The mitzva to separate at the expected time

- "*Ve-hizartem et benei Yisrael mi-tum'atam*" (Vayikra 15:31) → refrain when her period may come, lest it start during intimacy (Shevuot 18b).
- Right after: "*aḥarei mot shnei benei Aharon*" (16:1): even the great, with good intentions, who cross a line of holiness are punished (Nadav and Avihu).
- Ona brings the Shekhina (Sota 17a; Ramban, Iggeret Ha-kodesh); careless of the issur, it drives it away.
- A couple who sanctify themselves, joyful in ona and careful to separate, merit children who can restrain themselves and add blessing: sons fit to teach Torah, daughters of understanding (Shevuot 18b).

## 3. What's forbidden

- Only **intimacy** (the worry is the period starting then). **Hugging and kissing are permitted.** If they fear a hug may lead further (or to wasted seed), avoid that too.
- **End of the ona** → **internal bedika** (night ona → after she gets up) (4:3).
- Didn't check after a **fixed** veset or a **beinonit** → no intimacy until she checks and is clean (SA YD 189:4).

## 4. How long

- **Ona** = day (sunrise→sunset) or night (sunset→sunrise). Last period began by day → refrain by day; by night → by night.
- Even if it usually comes at the start or end, the **whole** ona; but not the ona before or after.
- Some say le-khatchila add the ona before. **Halakha:** the vast majority of Rishonim → **one ona** only.

## 5. No fixed cycle: three onot

- Calculated from the **last** period only (SA 189:1; Rema 13). Plus veset ha-guf (§7).
    1. **Onat ha-haflaga:** the interval between the last two periods (35 days last time → refrain on day 35 from the last one).
    2. **Ona beinonit:** **day 30** (the last period's start = day 1) = always **4 weeks + 1 day** (Sunday by day → Monday by day four weeks later).
    3. **Onat ha-ḥodesh:** the **same Hebrew date** next month (3rd of the month → 3rd).
        - Previous month 29 days → ḥodesh = beinonit. 30 days → ḥodesh is the day after beinonit.
- All three onot are by day if the last one started by day; by night if at night.

## 6. Fixed cycle

- **Veset kavua** = same pattern **3 times in a row**. Then only **one** ona.
    - **Haflaga:** e.g., 35 days, by day, three times → always day 35.
    - **Ḥodesh:** same Hebrew date and ona three times → that date each month.
- **Saw off-schedule** → next time three onot: (a) the fixed one, (b) ḥodesh and (c) haflaga from the new one. **No beinonit** (only for no fixed cycle). Off-schedule **3 times in a row** → fixed veset uprooted; back to §5.
- **Semi-fixed:** she doesn't know the day but knows it's **at most 29** or **at least 32** days → only **haflaga** (beinonit/ḥodesh fall on days she knows she won't see). Set by 3 consecutive cycles.

## 7. Veset ha-guf

- A distinct **physical sign** shortly before her period (cramps, back or head pain, heaviness) → next month when she feels it, refrain to the **end of that ona**, plus the three onot of §5 since it's not yet fixed (SA 189:19, 26).
    - Felt it and no period → no need to worry about it anymore.
    - **3 periods in a row** after that sign → fixed veset ha-guf; no other onot needed (Knesset Yeḥezkel 36).
    - Fixed, then she felt it and no period came → next month, veset ha-guf + ḥodesh + haflaga. **3 times** no period after the sign → uprooted (SA 189:19–26).
- Sign predicts the **next** ona → refrain then. Predicts 2–3 days ahead: clear ona → that one; one of two onot → both; one of three → not a veset ha-guf.
- **Stains** hours before her period (not tamei themselves, e.g., on colored cloth) can be a veset ha-guf: when they appear again, refrain to the end of the ona. Usually together with certain feelings, then only when with them; the feelings needn't be defined, as long as she knows the combination signals her period.

## 8. Some rules of vesatot

- A period lasts days (flow or on-and-off), but the ona is set by its **start** (SA 184:6; Rema 189:13).
- Vesatot are set by blood **with hargasha**, **not stains**, even tamei ones (Ra'avad; SA 190:54).
- **Lost track** of her onot (fixed or not) → permitted every day (perisha is d'rabbanan; safek d'rabbanan le-kula) (Ḥavvot Da'at 184:4; Ḥatam Sofer).
- To avoid that: **keep an exact record** of each period's start (and any special signs), to know when to refrain and whether a fixed veset is set.

## 9. Before a trip

- A husband leaving for several days must be with his wife **the night before** (ona's fixed times and when desire is greater). Same when she travels.
- In that merit they stay faithful in thought and deed: "*u-fakadeta navkha ve-lo teḥeta*" (Iyov 5:24).
- **Even if that night is an ona of perisha** → majority: be together (Torah ona overrides the Sages' perisha, as long as she hasn't actually felt her period). R. Tam and Rosh: refrain; just words of love. When possible, don't schedule a trip for an ona of perisha (Maharshal).
- Short trip, back before their usual ona → part with a hug and kiss.
- Unique to a trip; **not** on other nights, not even the night of tevila, during an ona of perisha.

## 10. Mesuleket damim (pregnancy and nursing)

- **Pregnant**: until confirmed by a reliable test, the first three months → keep onot as usual. Confirmed → **mesuleket damim**: no onot. (Early pregnancy bleeding can still make her tamei, but it isn't cyclical.)
- **Nursing**, even once a day → mesuleket damim, as long as **no period yet** and **< 24 months** since birth. Stopped nursing completely, saw a period, or 24 months passed → back to previous rules:
    - Fixed date before pregnancy (3rd of the month) → refrain then. Veset ha-guf → when felt, as before. Fixed haflaga → after seeing once, resumes. 3 times not at her fixed time → no longer.
    - No fixed veset → after seeing once, the three onot.

## 11. Hormonal pills

- Pills that suppress her period, and doctors expect none → **no onot** (fixed or not).
- After stopping → refrain at the ona when the period is expected. Pills and women vary, so learn your pattern. Until then, refrain from **48 hours** after the last pill. 3 times later (e.g., 3 days) → set that; in doubt, also the ona before.

## 12. Menopause

- **90 days** without a period → no more onot (even if she sees, it's not regular). She's tamei when she sees, but calculates no onot.
- After 90 days, **3 periods** in a fixed pattern → fixed veset (SA 189:30–31).
- Her **old fixed pattern** returns even **once** (e.g., the 3rd again) → back to her fixed veset (the 90 days weren't the final stop).
- After 90 days, 3 periods every month or two **without** a pattern → from the third, onot as for no fixed cycle.
